# Viral Short-Form Video Scriptwriter

## 1. The Anatomy of a High-Retention Short (30s - 60s)

A viral short-form script follows a strict 4-part pacing formula:

```text
[00:00 - 00:03] THE HOOK        -> Pattern Interrupt / Curiosity Gap (Stop the scroll)
[00:04 - 00:15] THE AGITATION   -> Why this matters, relatable pain, or stakes
[00:16 - 00:45] THE VALUE DROP  -> 1 to 3 concrete actionable steps or revelation
[00:46 - 00:60] THE FAST CTA    -> Natural closing loop and action request
```

---

## 2. The 3-Second Hook Formulas (Never Start Boring)

### ❌ What is FORBIDDEN:
- *"Halo teman-teman, balik lagi di channel gue..."* (Instant swipe-away).
- *"Di video kali ini gue mau ngebahas tentang..."* (Boring introduction).
- Slow title cards or channel logos before speaking.

### ✅ High-Retention Hook Archetypes:
1. **The Negative Warning Hook:**
   - *"Jangan pernah beli saham ini sebelum kamu tahu 1 rahasia ini..."*
   - *"Berhenti edit video dengan cara lama kalau gamau laptop kamu jebol!"*
2. **The Curiosity / "How to" Gap:**
   - *"Gimana cara bot ini download ribuan video TikTok tanpa watermark dalam 10 detik?"*
   - *"90% trader pemula ga sadar kalau indikator ini palsu..."*
3. **The Contrarian / Controversy Hook:**
   - *"Semua orang salah mikir kalau nabung saham itu selalu aman..."*
   - *"Kenapa aplikasi ini lebih cepat daripada CapCut?"*

## 3. Formatting Standards for Editors (Audio & Visual Cues)

Always write scripts with explicit visual and sound-effect directions embedded directly in brackets:

```text
[HOOK]
(Visual: Fast zoom-in ke layar hp, teks merah tebal "JANGAN BELI")
(Sound: Bass Drop / Vinyl scratch)
Narator: "Jangan pernah beli saham ini sebelum kamu tahu 1 rahasia ini!"

[AGITATION]
(Visual: B-roll grafik candlestick merah terjun bebas)
(Sound: Whoosh sound effect)
Narator: "Banyak orang fomo waktu harganya lagi di pucuk, padahal indikator RSI-nya udah overbought parah."

[VALUE]
(Visual: Kursor menyorot garis support di chart, muncul checklist point 1, 2, 3)
Narator: "Cukup cek 2 hal ini di dashboard kamu: pertama volume transaksi, kedua garis support mingguan."

[CTA & LOOP]
(Visual: Text overlay "Simpan video ini", panah menunjuk tombol bookmark)
Narator: "Simpan video ini biar ga nyesel pas market buka besok!"
```

---

## 4. Script Tone & Voice Optimization
- **Short Sentences:** Keep sentences under 12 words. Cut filler words (*jadi*, *sebenarnya*, *mungkin*).
- **Active Verbs:** Use dynamic action verbs.
- **Pacing:** Aim for ~130 to 150 words per 60 seconds (fast, confident, and energetic cadence).
- **The Infinite Loop:** Craft the final sentence to naturally flow right back into the first sentence (the hook) so looping views increase retention metrics.
